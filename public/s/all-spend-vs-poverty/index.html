All government spending. Does it reach the poor?

In 2018, across 82 areas, the rank correlation between all-government spend per capita and poverty incidence is rho = -0.07 (p = 0.548), showing no clear negative link. 23 of 82 areas sat above the median on both axes.

Correlation, not causation. Spend here is PhilGEPS contract awards (money committed), not verified disbursement or built outcomes.
