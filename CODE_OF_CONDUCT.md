# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

The full text is published at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, and contributions that do not align with the
Covenant, and may ban contributors for behavior they judge harmful.

## Reporting

To report a concern, email xpuspus@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
